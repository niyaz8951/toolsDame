# People and sign-in

One person is one account. Tools have no logins of their own.

## Three ways in
1. **New user** (IT admin page): IT makes the account with a username and a temporary password and tells them to the person by voice. At the first sign-in the site asks for a new password. Nothing opens before that. No email is needed.
2. **Add by work email**: IT approves an email address in advance. The person signs up himself with that address, chooses a password, and is in at once.
3. **Request access**: a person signs up on the start page and waits. An IT admin approves or refuses.

=> Account exists | First sign-in | Own password chosen | Hub opens

## Your account
- The round button at the top right opens **My account**: your details, your password, what you can open, your last sign-ins.
- You keep your job title, department, office and work phone up to date. Your name, sign-in and staff number are changed by IT only.
- You change your password there, with the password you have now.

## Forgot your password
- Ask an IT admin. He gives you a **temporary password** by voice and you are signed out everywhere.
- Sign in with it and choose a new password. Nothing opens before that. A temporary password counts for 7 days.

=> Ask IT | Temporary password by voice | Sign in | New password | Hub opens

## Who may ask
- IT sets the **work email domains**. An address outside them gets no request at all and leaves no trace.
- A new account is **pending** until approved. Pending means: every function refuses.

## While signed in
- One session per person, for 12 hours. Signing in somewhere else ends the older one.
- Every sign-in is written to the audit.

## When someone leaves
- IT switches the account off. He is out **at his next click**: tools, rows and files.
- The rows he added stay, still marked as his.

## What is a stand-in
> Email and password stand in for company sign-in. On the company platform, people sign in with their work account (with its second factor), and IT's directory decides who exists. The rest of the site does not change.
