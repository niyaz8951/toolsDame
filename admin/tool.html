How do you want to make the page of this tool?

Choose one. You can come back and choose again at any time; nothing is uploaded until the last step.

What should the tool do?

Take your time. Two or three sentences are enough, and you can change them later. The AI gets exactly these words. Put no real names, prices or customer data here.

Does the tool need to save data in the database?

The data will go into this site's database. You write no SQL and need no IT ticket: the AI lists the tables the tool needs, the site shows you that list in a later step, and it makes the tables when you press "Create the tables". You can see them at any time under Data on this page.

Anything else it must do? (optional)

Give the starter kit to the AI

The starter kit is one text: the rules of this site, a starting page and your description. Do these three things, in order:

  1. Press "Copy the starter kit".
  2. Open the approved AI, paste, and send it.
  3. Wait for its answer, then come back here and press Next.

The page you already have is inside the kit. The AI is asked to rebuild it for this site.

          A tool keeps its rows in tables of this site. Each table is drawn here like a sheet: one column per thing that is saved, and two made-up rows in grey so you see how a row looks. The site adds five columns of its own to every row (who added it and when); the button below shows them. The preview has its own empty copy of each table, so trying things there never touches real rows.
