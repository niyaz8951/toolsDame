Five steps, no programming. The site gives you a starter kit for the approved AI, checks what comes back and tells you what to fix. The look, the top bar, sign-in and security are already there: you bring only what the tool does.

  1. Say what the tool should do

    Saving rows that people share arrives in phase 4. Until then a tool can calculate, read files the person chooses, and give files back.

  2. Give the starter kit to the AI

    The starter kit is one text: the rules of this site, the building blocks, a starting page and your description. Copy it, paste it into the approved AI and send it. Put no real names, prices or customer data into the AI.
