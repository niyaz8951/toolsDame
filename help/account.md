# My account

1. **My details.** Your name, how you sign in, your staff number and your role. Only IT changes those. Below them you keep your own work details up to date: job title, department, office, work phone. Press **Save my details**.
2. **Password.** Type the password you have now, then the new one twice, and press **Change my password**. You stay signed in. The new password counts from your next sign-in.
3. **My access.** Every space and tool you can open, and what you may do in each. Missing something? Ask that space's admin or the tool's manager.
4. **My sign-ins.** Your last ten sign-ins. One you do not recognise: change your password at once and tell IT.

## Forgot your password
You cannot change a password you do not know. Ask an IT admin. He gives you a temporary password by voice. You sign in with it, type it once more, and choose a new password. Nothing opens before that. A temporary password counts for 7 days.

## Good to know
- You are signed in at one place at a time. A new sign-in ends the one before.
- A reset by IT signs you out everywhere at once.
- Everything on this page is yours only: nobody else's account can be opened from here.
