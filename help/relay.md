# Relay

Relay carries work from team to team along the value chain, for example a query from Sales to Application, to HQ-AES, and on to the Product team or the Factory. Each team is a space: its admins and members.

## My work
1. **With you**: what you picked up. Open it, do your part.
2. **Waiting for your teams**: nobody has it yet. Open it and press **Pick it up**, so the others know you are on it.
3. **You started**: what you began, now with other teams. You can follow it and write a note.

## Doing your part
1. Tick every line of the checklist. Each tick is saved at once.
2. Then choose one:
   - **Pass on**: to the next team. A stage marked "if needed" may be skipped.
   - **Answer back**: the answer goes to the team that passed it to you, and straight to the person there who did.
   - **Something is missing**: it goes back to that same team, with what you wrote.
   - **Close it**: at the last stage, or at the first team once the answer has come back.
3. Not your turn after all: **Put it back for the team**.

Each level answers only the level that asked it: an item always goes back the way it came.

## Flows
Each flow is drawn as its chain of teams, from left to right. Under each team: what is there now, what is late, how long it usually takes, and how often it sent something back. Many send-backs show which hand-over needs a better checklist.

## Who may do what
- Only the team holding an item can move it. The database checks this, not the page.
- An admin of the team may take over an item from a colleague who is away.
- Who started it, an admin of the first team, or IT may cancel it, with a reason.
- Everyone on the flow sees its items and their history. Nothing in the history can be changed or deleted.

## For IT: setting up a flow
1. **Set up** > **Add a flow**, or start from an example.
2. Give it a name, what one item is called, and the letters of its number (MTO gives MTO-0001).
3. Add the stages in order. For each: the team (a space), a name, the hours it should take at most, the checklist, and whether it may be skipped.
4. **Create the flow**. The team of the first stage can now start items.

While items are open, stages can be renamed and checklists changed, but not added, removed, moved or given to another team. A flow is never deleted: switch it off and nothing new starts, while open items still finish.

Relay changes no space, tool or table. Tools work exactly as before.
