# IT admin

1. **Waiting for approval.** People who asked for access wait here. Approve only people you know; check the email address.
2. **Add a person.** Write a name and a work email. The person then signs up on the start page with exactly that email, chooses his own password, and is in at once. You never see a password. "Take back" undoes it as long as he has not signed up.
3. **People.** Switch a leaver off here: he loses everything at his next click. Make someone IT admin here. You cannot change your own account.
4. **Site settings.** Who may ask for access (the work email domains), and the AI prompt button.
5. **Spaces.** Create a space for a department, give it an id (permanent, never used again) and name its first admin. That admin then makes the tools.
6. **Audit.** Who did what, when. Nothing here can be changed or deleted.

## Good to know
- Being IT admin does not open a department's data. To look inside a space, name yourself its admin; that is written in the audit.
- A person added here still has to prove the email address is his (the confirmation email), unless that was switched off in the sign-in service.
