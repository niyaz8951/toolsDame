# Site book

This book is written for IT. The hub shows it to IT admins only, and this page opens it for IT admins only.

1. Press a chapter on the left to read it. "Next chapter" at the bottom goes on.
2. The book has four parts: the overview, the detail, what a reviewer asks, and the log. **Present the overview**, **Present the detail** or **Present this chapter** shows that part as slides.
3. In the presentation: Next and Back, or the arrow keys and the space bar. Esc closes it. "Full screen" hides everything else.

The book is kept up to date with the site: every change adds a line to the last chapter, "Notes and changes".

The chapters are files of the site, like its pages. When IT moves the site to its own servers, the folder `book/` is closed there to everyone but IT.
