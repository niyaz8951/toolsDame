# Site book

1. Press a chapter on the left to read it. "Next chapter" at the bottom goes on.
2. The book has three parts: the overview for everyone, the detail for IT, and the log. **Present the overview**, **Present the detail for IT** or **Present this chapter** shows that part as slides.
3. In the presentation: Next and Back, or the arrow keys and the space bar. Esc closes it. "Full screen" hides everything else.

The book is kept up to date with the site: every change adds a line to the last chapter, "Notes and changes".
